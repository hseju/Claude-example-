Controls

Desktop: WASD or arrow keys to walk, click the ground to walk there, drag to turn the camera, scroll to zoom, Q/E to rotate, M for the map, Enter to go into a hall. In the hall, keys 1–6 perform the prayer actions.
Touch: joystick or tap to walk, drag to turn, pinch to zoom.
