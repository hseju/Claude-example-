# Audio credits

Music plays as three stations, chosen from the sound panel (the speaker button): **Uplifting** (feel-good grooves, the default by day), **Meditation** (calm, the default at night) and **Classical flute**. Tracks play whole, shuffled, and crossfade into each other. Under the music runs a quiet bed of nature sound: a mountain stream with a songbird by day, crickets at night.

Every file is an edit of a recording published under a permissive licence: trimmed, faded, level-matched (about −18 LUFS for music) and, for the ambience, filtered and crossfaded into a seamless loop. Files derived from CC BY-SA recordings are themselves licensed **CC BY-SA** under the same terms.
