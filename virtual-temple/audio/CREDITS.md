# Audio credits

The soundscape is meant to be calm: solo bamboo flute for the day, singing bowls at night, and a soft bed of birdsong or crickets underneath. Every file here is an edit of a recording published under a permissive licence: trimmed, filtered, loudness-normalised and crossfaded into a seamless loop.

Files derived from CC BY-SA recordings are themselves licensed **CC BY-SA** under the same terms.

## Music

The three flute pieces are the solo flute alapanas (free, unmetered raga exposition, no percussion) from one live Carnatic concert recorded at the National Centre for Biological Sciences, Bengaluru, and published by Music@NCBS on Jamendo.
